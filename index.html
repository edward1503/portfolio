Air Quality Pipeline

From raw measurements to analytics-ready data

An AWS Medallion pipeline combining WAQI measurements and historical Vietnamese air-quality data for analysis.

Quality gates before analytics.Bronze → Silver → Gold · Athena checks · Step Functions
